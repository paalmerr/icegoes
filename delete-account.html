Delete your account

Last updated: 24 November 2026

You can delete your Twizzle account and all its game data at any time. Deletion is immediate and cannot be undone.

In the app

Open Profile, tap "Delete account", then "Delete everything". Your account, picks, Ice XP, display name, friends and recovery code are erased at once.

Without the app

Write to icegoess@gmail.com with the subject "Delete account" and your Player ID (shown in Profile once you saved progress, for example K7QM-2XRA). Never send your recovery code. We delete the account within 30 days and reply when it is done.

A guest account with no saved progress cannot be found without the phone it lives on: delete it in the app, or simply uninstall the app and the account stays empty and unused.

What is deleted and what is kept

Everything linked to the account is deleted. Nothing is kept, except that short-lived server logs and backups expire on their own schedule. See the Privacy Policy for details.
