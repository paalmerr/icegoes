Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 November 2026

Twizzle is built to know as little about you as possible. You play without an email, name or phone number. This policy explains the little we do keep, why, and how to delete it.

1. Who is responsible

Ice Goes, Spain, is responsible for your data in Twizzle (the data controller under the GDPR). Contact: icegoess@gmail.com.

2. What we do not collect

No email, name, phone number, date of birth, location, contacts, photos or advertising ID. No ads, no tracking across apps or websites, no analytics tools, no push notifications. We never sell or rent data.

3. What we keep

A random account ID, created when you first open the app.

Your game: picks, when you locked them, Ice XP, level, streaks and league places, your avatar look, favourite skaters, the stars you earn and give, items from the shop, and the days you open the app (for the daily star).

What you choose to add: a display name for leagues, your friend code and the friends you add.

If you save progress: a login made from the first part of your recovery code and a protected (hashed) form of the code. We cannot read your code afterwards.

Technical logs: our hosting provider records requests (IP address, time, device or browser type) for security and to stop abuse. These logs are deleted automatically after a short time.

4. On your phone only

Your sign-in session and your language choice are stored on your device and never leave it except to sign you in.

5. Why we use it

To run the game you asked for: save picks, score them, show leagues and friends, and restore progress with your code. Logs are used to keep the service secure. Legal basis (where the GDPR applies): performing our agreement with you (the Terms of Use) and our legitimate interest in security.

6. What other players see

In leagues: your display name (or "Skater" with a few letters), level and season Ice XP. Friends you add see the same. Other players never see your picks, your recovery code or your account ID. Only the people who run Twizzle can look at game data, to fix problems.

7. Services we use

Supabase stores the data on servers in the European Union (Ireland) and runs our sign-in.

Pictures load straight from their hosts: skater photos from Wikimedia Commons, video previews from YouTube, podcast covers from the podcast host. Like any website, these hosts see your IP address when a picture loads. Episodes and videos open in their own apps and websites (for example Yandex Music, Apple Podcasts, YouTube) under their privacy policies.

8. How long we keep it

Until you delete your account. Delete it in Profile: your account and game data are erased at once and cannot be restored. Copies in backups disappear when those backups expire.

We may delete guest accounts with no saved progress after a long time without use.

9. Your rights

You can see your game data in the app, change your display name, and delete everything in Profile. For other requests (a copy of your data, corrections, objections) write to icegoess@gmail.com with your Player ID from Profile. Never send your recovery code. You can also complain to a data protection authority, for example the Spanish Data Protection Agency (AEPD, aepd.es) or the one where you live.

10. Children

Twizzle is for people aged 14 and over (the age of digital consent in Spain). We do not knowingly collect data from younger children; if you think a child under 14 uses the app, contact us and we will delete the account.

11. Security

Data travels encrypted (HTTPS). Database rules let each player read and change only their own game data. The recovery code is stored only in hashed form.

12. Changes

If we change how we handle data, we update this policy and the date at the top, and tell you in the app about important changes.
